Convertly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Convertly ("the app", "Convertly", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Convertly Android application.

1. File Processing

Convertly is designed to perform file conversions directly on your device. Files selected for conversion, including images, PDFs, and Base64 data, are processed locally on your device.

Convertly does not require you to upload your files to our own servers for conversion. We do not intentionally collect or store the contents of files you convert.

2. Advertising

The free version of Convertly may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about advertising interactions in accordance with Google's policies and applicable privacy requirements.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Convertly uses Google's consent mechanisms where required to obtain and manage consent for personalized advertising and related processing.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy

3. Google Play and Pro Purchases

Convertly may offer a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play. Payments and payment information are handled by Google Play. Convertly does not receive or store your full payment card or banking details.

Google may process purchase information necessary to complete and verify your transaction.

4. Information We Collect

Convertly itself does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your name, address, phone number, contacts, or the contents of files you convert.

Third-party services integrated into the app, such as Google AdMob and Google Play, may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Device Permissions

Convertly may request access to files or media on your device when you choose a file to convert or save a converted file. Access is used for the conversion functionality you request.

6. Data Retention

Files processed by Convertly remain under your control on your device. Convertly does not intentionally upload or retain converted file contents on our own servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Convertly is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For advertising-related choices, you may also use the privacy and consent controls provided within Convertly where available.

9. Third-Party Services

Convertly may use the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Play services for application distribution and purchases

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
